In Detail

Buckingham Palace

Location: The UK

Price: $25,000,000

Buckingham Palace, located in London, is the official residence of the British monarch. Originally built in 1703 for the Duke of Buckingham, it became the royal residence in 1837 during Queen Victoria’s reign. The palace is renowned for its neoclassical architecture, featuring a grand façade, lavish interiors, and expansive gardens. It serves as both a home to the royal family and the venue for important state occasions and ceremonies. The Changing of the Guard ceremony, held regularly at the palace, is a major tourist attraction. Buckingham Palace remains an enduring symbol of British monarchy and heritage.

Burj Khalifa

Location: UAE

Price: $15,000,000

The Burj Khalifa, located in Dubai, UAE, is the tallest building in the world, standing at 828 meters (2,717 feet). Completed in 2010, it was designed by architect Adrian Smith and built by the firm Emaar Properties. The tower's sleek, modern design is inspired by Islamic architecture, with a series of setbacks that create its distinctive silhouette. It features luxury residences, corporate offices, and a hotel. The Burj Khalifa also boasts an observation deck with breathtaking views of Dubai. Its construction symbolizes Dubai’s rapid growth and ambition to become a global city of innovation and luxury.

Casa Batlló

Location: Spain

Price: $10,000,000

Casa Batlló is a landmark building in Barcelona, Spain, designed by the famous architect Antoni Gaudí. Completed in 1906, it showcases the Catalan Modernisme style with its whimsical, organic forms. The façade is a vibrant mosaic of colorful tiles, with curved lines and shapes that evoke nature, such as bone-like balconies and a dragon-inspired roof. The interior features flowing spaces and imaginative designs, including intricate woodwork and stained glass. Casa Batlló is a UNESCO World Heritage site and one of Gaudí’s most celebrated works, drawing visitors from around the world for its unique beauty.

Fallingwater

Location: The USA

Price: $5,000,000

Fallingwater is an iconic house designed by architect Frank Lloyd Wright, completed in 1939 in rural Pennsylvania. Built over a waterfall, the house is a prime example of Wright's philosophy of organic architecture, which seeks to harmonize buildings with their natural surroundings. The design features cantilevered terraces that extend over the waterfall, allowing the structure to blend seamlessly with the landscape. Made of stone, steel, and glass, Fallingwater's open, airy interiors reflect Wright's commitment to connecting the indoors with nature. The house is a National Historic Landmark and one of the most famous examples of American residential architecture.

Mar-a-Lago

Location: The USA

Price: $10,000,000

Mar-a-Lago is a historic estate located in Palm Beach, Florida, originally built between 1924 and 1927 by heiress Marjorie Merriweather Post. Designed by architect Marion Sims Wyeth in a Mediterranean Revival style, the mansion features luxurious interiors, sprawling gardens, and a private beach. In 1985, it was purchased by Donald Trump, who later transformed it into a private club and residence. Mar-a-Lago became widely known as a retreat for Trump, hosting political and social events. It has since become a symbol of wealth and exclusivity, often associated with both Trump’s business ventures and political activities.

Trump Tower

Location: India

Price: $2,000,000

Trump Tower Gurgaon (now known as Trump Tower Delhi NCR) is a luxury residential project located in Gurgaon, Haryana, just outside New Delhi, India. Developed by the Tribeca Developers in collaboration with the Trump Organization, it offers high-end, ultra-modern apartments with world-class amenities. The tower's design features sleek glass facades, spacious layouts, and panoramic views of the surrounding cityscape. With 6-star services, it includes features like a private elevator access, swimming pools, a fitness center, and concierge services. The project is targeted at high-net-worth individuals seeking luxury living in one of India's most prestigious real estate markets.

White House

Location: The USA

Price: $10,000,000

The White House is the official residence and workplace of the President of the United States, located at 1600 Pennsylvania Avenue in Washington, D.C. Designed by James Hoban in the neoclassical style, it was completed in 1800 and has served as the residence of every U.S. president since John Adams. The White House consists of several wings, including the Executive Residence, the West Wing (housing the Oval Office), and the East Wing. It is a symbol of U.S. government and power, hosting state dinners, diplomatic events, and serving as the headquarters of the U.S. executive branch. Its iconic design and role in American politics make it one of the most recognized buildings in the world.
